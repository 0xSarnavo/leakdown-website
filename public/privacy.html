Privacy policy

Last updated 14 September 2026.

This site and the run-request service are operated by Sarnavo Saha Sardar, an individual based in India. Questions and requests go to sssarnavo@gmail.com. The open-source tool itself runs on your own computer and sends nothing here; the last section covers it.

What this website collects

  • Nothing, for visitors. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts, no external fonts. The hosting provider keeps ordinary web-server logs (IP address, path, time) under its own retention.
  • A run request, if you send one: the site URL you typed, your email address, the fact that you ticked the authorisation box, and the time. That is one record in a private object-storage bucket on Railway (US West region), readable only with a token held by the operator.
  • Rate limiting keeps request counts per IP address in the server's memory for one minute. They are never written to disk.

What a requested run produces

The operator starts the run by hand on their own computer. Simulated prospects visit the site you named in a real browser. The run produces screenshots, a screen recording, a step-by-step log of what each prospect saw and decided, generated prospect profiles with fictional one-word names, and the report. If your site sends verification emails, they go to throwaway addresses on a domain the operator controls and are read by the tool to extract codes; those emails sit in the operator's mailbox. Anything visible on your site's pages during the run, including any personal data your own pages display, appears in those screenshots and logs.

All of that is stored on the operator's computer, not on this website. The report is sent to the email address you gave, as a PDF.

Who else sees data

  • Railway hosts this site and the request bucket.
  • Google delivers the report email from the operator's Gmail account.
  • The AI provider whose model plays the prospects (Anthropic through Claude Code, or another provider the operator chooses) receives the text and screenshots of your site's pages during the run, under that provider's terms. The prospects' email addresses are throwaway.

Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to contact you about anything other than your request.

How long it is kept

Until you ask. Email sssarnavo@gmail.com from the address you used and the request record, the run folder, the throwaway mailboxes and the sent copy of the report are deleted within 30 days. You can also ask what is held about you, or for a correction, the same way.

Site names in public

A site that was tested is named publicly only with its owner's written consent. Until then it appears under a placeholder such as "site-g".

Age

The service is for people who run or are authorised to test a website. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

The open-source tool

When you run client-simulator yourself, nothing is sent to this website. Sessions, recordings and reports stay on your machine. Your chosen AI provider sees the pages your prospects visit, and if you configure a mailbox, verification emails land in it. What that provider keeps is governed by your agreement with them.

Changes

Changes are made here, with the date at the top. Material changes to what is collected are also noted on the request form.
